## 2. Introduction
TaskFunds is a digital marketplace that connects businesses with individual who complete online tasks for rewards. The platform focuses on transperency; trust, and fair opportuinities by providing verified campaigns, reputations system, secure payment, and professional user profiles.

## 3. Mission
to create a trusted structured platform where businesses achieve real results through reliable workers while empowering individuals to earn income through legitimate digital opportinuties.

## 4. Vision
to become African's leading trusted task marketplace, connecting millions of workers and business through transperency, innovation and security.
